## 4. Gaps common to all case studies

1. **Individual role** in each team project (what Mikel personally did).
2. **Original image files**: renders, CAD screenshots and sketches at full resolution. The images embedded in Word documents are often compressed.
3. **Tools per project** (SolidWorks / NX / KeyShot / Blender aren't named in most documents).
4. **Permission** to show teammates' work, and how to credit them.
5. **English copy**: 6 of 10 sources are in Spanish or Basque. Case-study text will be newly written in English from these sources.
